## Equation and units

Equation 3, journal page 428, is `A(P)=Am*[1/2+atan((P-P0)/P1)/pi]`. P is transmural pressure. Am is asymptotic maximal cross-sectional area; P0 is the pressure of maximal area compliance; P1 is its pressure half-width. Thus `C(P)=Am/[pi*P1*(1+((P-P0)/P1)^2)]`, and C(P0 +/- P1)=C(P0)/2. A physical curve requires Am>0 and P1>0; its inverse has domain 0<A<Am.

Table 2 uses Am in cm^2 and P0/P1 in mmHg. SI conversion: 1 cm^2=1e-4 m^2 and 1 mmHg=133.322 Pa. Negative fitted P0 entries below are present in the original table.

## Measurement conditions

Journal pages 426-427 describe autopsy samples from 45 thoracic and 20 abdominal aortas, ages 30-88 years, including 13 subject pairs. Thoracic samples were approximately 10 cm of descending aorta just above the diaphragm; abdominal samples were approximately 6 cm between the renal arteries and iliac bifurcation. Segments were mounted at their precise in-situ length.

The perfusate was oxygenated Tyrode solution, pH 7.4 +/- 0.1, at 37 +/- 1 degrees C. Pressure rose from 20 to 180 mmHg in 20 mmHg steps, then fell by corresponding steps. Diameter recording began before each step and continued at least 50 seconds after onset; diameter was usually stable within 1 micrometre at that point. These are static measurements. Table 2 parameter estimates use the inflation measurements.
